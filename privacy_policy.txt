                           PRIVACY POLICY - ASK GAMES HUB

Thank you for choosing Ask Games Hub. Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy
Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information
when you use our mobile game application. Please read this policy carefully. By
using Ask Games Hub, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree
to be bound by the terms of this Privacy Policy.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We collect information in the following ways to provide and improve your gaming
experience:

1.1 Information You Provide Directly
-------------------------------------
- Account Information: When you create or edit your profile, we collect your
  chosen display name and avatar/emoji.
- User Content: Any custom profile settings or preferences you choose to save
  within the app.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically
----------------------------------------
- Game Data: Game scores, levels, XP (experience points), coins, gems, tokens,
  and in-game progress generated as you play.
- Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device
  identifiers, app version, and screen resolution.
- Usage Data: Which games you play, duration of sessions, feature interactions,
  general gameplay patterns, and app launch events.
- Performance Data: App crash logs, error reports, and performance metrics used
  to improve stability.

1.3 Leaderboard & Social Features
----------------------------------
- Leaderboard Data: Your display name, avatar, scores, and rank are visible on
  Global, Friends, and Country leaderboards.
- Achievements: Unlocked achievements, timestamps, and associated scores are
  stored and displayed on your profile.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- Provide & Maintain Services: Operate the games, sync your progress across
  sessions, store your profile, preferences, and rewards data.
- Leaderboards & Rankings: Display your scores, achievements, and ranks
  alongside other players.
- Rewards & Progression: Track daily task completion, distribute daily rewards
  (coins, gems, etc.), and calculate level progression (XP).
- Improve the App: Analyze which games and features are popular, identify bugs,
  optimize performance, and develop new content.
- Personalization: Customize your in-app experience, show relevant game
  suggestions, and surface achievements tailored to your activity.
- Customer Support: Respond to inquiries, troubleshoot issues, and provide
  assistance if you contact us via Help & Support.
- Communications: Send push notifications (only if you opt in) regarding
  rewards, tournaments, updates, and important service announcements.
- Security: Detect and prevent fraud, cheating, abuse, or unauthorized access
  to our systems.
- Legal Compliance: Comply with applicable laws, regulations, and lawful
  government requests.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. DATA STORAGE & RETENTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Your profile, progress, scores, achievements, and reward data are stored
  locally on your device and may be synced to secure cloud servers operated by
  us or our trusted service providers (such as Google Firebase).
- We retain your account data for as long as your account is active or as needed
  to provide services. If you request account deletion, we will remove or
  anonymize your data within 30 days, unless a longer retention period is
  required by law.
- Analytics and performance logs are retained for up to 12 months before being
  anonymized or deleted.

LOCAL STORAGE NOTE:
Most of your game progress, settings (music, sound effects, vibration, language
preferences), and profile data is stored locally on your device using the app's
private storage. Removing the app from your device will delete this locally
stored data.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. HOW WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only in the
following circumstances:

4.1 Displayed to Other Players
-------------------------------
- Your chosen display name, avatar, scores, and ranks are inherently visible to
  other players through the Global, Friends, and Country leaderboards.
- If you participate in multiplayer games or tournaments, your display name,
  avatar, and moves/score will be visible to matched opponents.

4.2 Trusted Service Providers
------------------------------
- Cloud & Hosting: We may use providers such as Google Cloud, Firebase, or
  similar services to host user data, sync progress, and deliver features.
  These providers process data only as instructed by us and under strict
  confidentiality obligations.
- Analytics: Tools such as Google Analytics for Firebase may be used to
  understand app usage and improve product quality.
- Advertising Partners: If the app displays advertisements, our ad partners
  (e.g., Google AdMob) may collect device and usage information to serve
  relevant ads. Their collection and use of data is governed by their own
  privacy policies.
- Payment Processors: If you make in-app purchases, the Google Play Store
  handles all transactions and payment details directly. We do not process or
  store your credit card or financial information.

4.3 Legal & Safety
-------------------
- We may disclose information if required to do so by law, subpoena, or
  government request.
- We may disclose information when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect
  our rights, your safety, the safety of users, or to investigate fraud,
  security incidents, or violations of our Terms of Service.
- In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be
  transferred as part of that business transaction.

5. ADVERTISING & THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The app may contain advertising. Third-party ad networks may collect and use
information about your device and in-app activity to serve personalized or
non-personalized ads. This may include:

- Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID / AAID on Android)
- IP address and approximate geographic location (country-level)
- In-game event data for ad performance measurement

On Android devices, you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad
personalization via your device's Settings -> Privacy -> Ads menu.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services
integrated into the app for more information about their practices.

6. YOUR RIGHTS & CHOICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the EU, UK, California, Brazil, and
others), you may have rights regarding your personal information. These may
include:

- Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
- Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data. You may also
  update your display name and avatar directly in the app's Profile or Settings.
- Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated personal data. Note
  that some data may be retained if legally required.
- Restriction: Request restriction of processing of your data under certain
  circumstances.
- Objection: Object to processing of your data for direct marketing or for
  purposes based on legitimate interests.
- Data Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format for transfer
  to another provider.
- Withdraw Consent: Withdraw any previously given consent (e.g., push
  notifications) at any time through your device settings or the in-app Settings
  menu.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details provided in Section 12
(Contact Us). We will respond to valid requests within 30 days. In some cases,
we may request verification of identity before processing sensitive requests.
